07 — About

Visual / UI Designer & AI Creative Designer.

I'm Umar Daraz, a multidisciplinary visual and UI designer and AI creative designer. My work moves between brand and social campaigns, websites and e-commerce, mobile apps and interactive concepts.

It began with game UI studies, grew through an internship at NS Marketing and design work for LemdaTech, and now includes AI-assisted creative exploration. I have also created design work for Playnex Studio.
